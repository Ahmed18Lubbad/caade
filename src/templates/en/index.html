About us

The Cooperative Association for Agricultural Development and Environment – Gaza (CAADE) is an independent Palestinian cooperative association that works to promote sustainable economic, social, and environmental development and empower members, farmers, and local communities. It operates on the principles of cooperative work and the social and solidarity economy by developing innovative cooperative economic models and strengthening local production, value chains, and marketing.

The Association adopts an integrated approach that combines humanitarian response, early recovery, and sustainable development, with a focus on food security, restoring and developing agricultural production and livelihoods, economic empowerment, sustainable agricultural and environmental solutions, efficient use of resources, and climate adaptation. It serves members and non-members, prioritizing farmers, women, youth, and the most vulnerable and affected households.

02

Establishment and origins

The Cooperative Association for Agricultural Development and Environment – Gaza (CAADE) was established in 2021 by professionals with experience in agricultural development, cooperative work, environmental management, and development and humanitarian projects. CAADE is an independent Palestinian cooperative association officially registered with the General Directorate of Cooperatives under registration number 1696, with legal personality and financial and administrative independence in accordance with Palestinian cooperative legislation.

03

Our mission

We serve members and the local community and strengthen our institutional capacities through innovative cooperative economic models, the development of agricultural production and value chains, cooperative marketing, modern technologies, and sustainable development programmes aligned with the principles of the social and solidarity economy. We promote participation, justice, solidarity, and sustainability while supporting agricultural recovery, resilience, improved livelihoods, and environmental sustainability.

04

Our values

Justice and equal opportunities; transparency and accountability; excellence and quality; innovation; sustainability; solidarity and cooperation; partnership; empowerment and participation.

05

Our vision

A pioneering cooperative association that strengthens the resilience and empowerment of members and the local community, and serves as an innovative cooperative model for sustainable agricultural development.
